DELGRAPHYHA / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Delgraphyha Shorts Factory is a private production tool that its owner uses to prepare short videos and publish them to the owner's own channels.

Who operates it

The application is operated by its owner, Delgraphyha. It is not offered to the public and has no public registration. Contact: alimodiriirani@gmail.com

What Google / YouTube data is accessed

The application requests one Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. With it, the application can upload videos to the YouTube channel of the owner who signs in and approves access. It does not read the channel's videos, comments, analytics, subscribers or any other account data, and it does not access Gmail, Drive or other Google services.

Why access is needed

To upload videos that the owner has created and explicitly approved, to the owner's own YouTube channel, at scheduled times. Nothing is uploaded without the owner's prior approval of that specific video.

How tokens are stored and protected

The OAuth refresh token is stored on a single computer controlled by the owner, in a file that only the application's service account can read. It is not shown in the dashboard, not written to logs and not sent to any other service. The application contacts Google's token endpoint only to refresh access.

Sharing with third parties

Google user data is not sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to third parties, and is not used for advertising or for training machine-learning models. The only transfer is the upload of the owner's own video file to YouTube.

Retention and deletion

The token is kept until the owner disconnects the account. The owner can disconnect at any time by revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and by deleting the stored token file on the application's computer. After revocation the application can no longer access the channel. Videos already uploaded remain on YouTube until the owner deletes them there.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Delgraphyha Shorts Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other platforms

The owner may connect other platforms separately, each under its own authorisation and its own terms. This policy describes the Google / YouTube connection.

Website visitors

This website has no forms, accounts, cookies or analytics. The hosting provider may process technical request data (such as IP address) as part of delivering pages.
