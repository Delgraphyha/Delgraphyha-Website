DELGRAPHYHA / SHORTS-FACTORY

Shorts Factory

Delgraphyha Shorts Factory is the owner's private tool for producing short videos.

What it does

It prepares narrated or musical short videos from texts, music and footage owned by the owner. Every video is listened to and approved by the owner before anything is published.

YouTube integration

After approval, a video is uploaded to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API using the youtube.upload scope. The application does not read any other data from the channel. See the Privacy Policy for details on how access is stored and how to revoke it.

Availability

This is not a public multi-user service. There is no registration or login on this website.
