Rain on the Window

Every night, Grandmother sat by the window. When it rained, she turned off the light and simply listened.

One night her grandson asked, “Why do you turn off the light?”
She smiled. “Because the rain is playing music. When the light is on, the eyes stay awake and the ears fall asleep.”

The boy sat down beside her. At first he heard only drops: fast, slow, fast again. Slowly he understood that each drop had its own place: one on the glass, one in the gutter, one on the leaves of the fig tree in the yard.

Years later, in a distant, rainy city, he missed home one night. He turned off the light and sat by the window. The rain was still playing the same song.

For everyone far from home who can still hear the rain.
